COMMAND FLOW

1. RGB camera captures an image.
2. Onboard computer receives and preprocesses the frame.
3. Vision estimates free space, obstacles, and goal direction.
4. State estimator combines visual motion with encoder/IMU feedback.
5. Local risk map labels drivable / uncertain / blocked regions.
6. Local planner chooses a short safe trajectory.
7. Confidence-aware safety supervisor checks the planned motion.
8. Motion controller converts desired linear/angular motion into left/right wheel speeds.
9. Onboard computer sends a compact JSON command to the MCU.
10. MCU converts target wheel speeds into a closed-loop motor command.
11. MCU outputs PWM + direction to the H-bridge.
12. H-bridge drives the DC motors.
13. Encoders report wheel motion and the cycle repeats.
