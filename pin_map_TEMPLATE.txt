Left encoder A  -> interrupt-capable pin

Ultrasonic TRIG -> digital output
Ultrasonic ECHO -> digital input

IMPORTANT:
- Confirm H-bridge logic levels and motor current rating.
- Common ground between MCU and motor driver.
- Never power motors directly from the MCU.
